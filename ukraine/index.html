    Ukraine war has had a considerable impact on my life since it began over 4 years ago. At that time I was completely ignorant to wars and geopolitics. These topics never interested me enough to look into them before, however reading about it day after day in newspaper and watching it on news spiked my curiosity because my mind could not comprehend the events and how long it had been ongoing at that time.

    My first proper exposure to the Ukraine War was from a YouTube channel called WarLeaks which provided helmet cam footage of soldiers and drone footage of the events unfolding at the front lines. The POV at the time felt like a Call of Duty game and it challenged my understanding of war at the time. My first thought was if information and communications are such a crucial aspect of wars why would they make so much sensitive footage publically available which could be easily accessed by their adversary when as far back as WWll the Germans were employing the Enigma Machine to encrypt their communications.

    I would spend the next 3 years searching for an answer to many questions including what is the true nature of war. It always felt like I never progressed any closer to the answer until one day it all just comes together from the knowledge I have been acquiring.

    "War is a buisness" is what I found out and it made me wonder why would it take me so long to understand something so simple. It also sheds light on how censored the intenet has really become.
