# Democracy

Democracies like science are self correcting. You cast your vote and if the goverment fails with its policies you can accept that you made a mistake and cast your vote for someone else. The new regime may have better policies.

The dictatorships such as of Russia and Turkey have no such self correcting mechanism. If Putin makes a poor decision for their country, no mechanism exists to fix it.


## The ~~Democracy~~ Autocracy of India

Many people still believe that India is a democratic country.

Democracy does not exist anymore in India.
It has been replaced by **Electoral Autocracy**, a form of governance where illusion of election ensures masses believe in the existance of a democratic system when in reality all the foundationally important institutions are owned and controlled by the regime including the electoral commission and the supreme court.
