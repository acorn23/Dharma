Observe that there is a fractal pattern to Ponzi schemes: at scale, Ponzi schemes include corporations, real estate, financial markets, currencies, entire civilizations, and life itself. There is a selfish gene which motivates people to have children, so that they can have children, and ensuring the continuity of some genetic self-being.

The modern reader may be familiar with boomer memes, inter-generational conflict, the unaffordability of living and crippling sovereign debt. That is because civilization is a long con, played out over generations. Without more new victims, the system implodes on itself, as the debt can no longer be passed onto the future. Many young people understand the raw deal they are given and choose not to have children, and it is not just about suffering economic conditions or destruction of the natural environment, which are common rationalizations for a subconscious understanding of their situation. It is a subconscious realization that human civilization is headed down a dark path with no worthwhile future, due to the increasingly inhumane social contract of every developed nation state.

It was never about creating a better world for anyone but themselves.

The era of individual autonomy, while useful in limited degree, has ended and most civilizations are coming to terms with it. Turns out that most people would give up essential liberty for temporary security, but end up with neither, as Benjamin Franklin suggested. Most NPCs distrust their peers more than their government, leading to government overreach in everyone's lives.
